ContactFlow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 15, 2026

What data we collect

ContactFlow does not collect, transmit, or share any data with external servers. All contact information you save is stored locally in your browser only.

How it works

When you click "Save Contact" on a LinkedIn profile page, the extension reads the publicly visible profile information displayed on your screen and saves it to your browser's local storage. This is equivalent to manually copying information you can already see.

Data stored locally

  • Contact names, headlines, locations, and companies from profiles you choose to save
  • Email addresses visible on the profile page
  • Profile URLs

No external communication

This extension makes zero network requests. No data ever leaves your browser. No analytics, no tracking, no telemetry.

Permissions explained

  • activeTab: To read profile information on the LinkedIn page you're viewing
  • storage: To save your contacts locally

Data deletion

You can delete all saved data at any time by clicking "Clear All" in the extension popup, or by uninstalling the extension.
