TubeSEO — Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 15, 2026

What data we collect

TubeSEO does not collect, transmit, or share any data. All analysis is performed locally in your browser.

How it works

When you analyze a YouTube video, the extension reads publicly visible metadata (title, tags, description length, etc.) from the YouTube page you're viewing and calculates an SEO score locally. No data is sent anywhere.

Data stored locally

  • Analysis history (last 50 videos analyzed) — titles, scores, timestamps
  • Total analysis count

No external communication

This extension makes zero network requests. Everything runs in your browser.

Permissions explained

  • activeTab: To read video metadata on the YouTube page
  • storage: To save analysis history locally
